11. **C** — 31 has no positive factors other than 1 and 31.
12. **B** — 49 = 40 + 9, written XL + IX = XLIX.
13. **D** — The tens digit is 4, so 6,748 rounds down to 6,700.
14. **C** — The sequence increases by 5; 20 + 5 = 25.
15. **B** — The digits of 4,218 sum to 15, a multiple of 3.
16. **B** — Divide numerator and denominator of 6/12 by 6 to get 1/2.

18. **B** — 7/8 − 3/8 = 4/8 = 1/2.
19. **B** — 24 ÷ 3 = 8.
20. **B** — Multiplying 3/4 by 3/3 gives 9/12.
21. **A** — 0.6 = 6/10, which simplifies to 3/5.
