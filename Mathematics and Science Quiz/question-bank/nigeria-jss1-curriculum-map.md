# Nigeria JSS 1 — Working Curriculum Map

**Status:** Subject selection draft; verify against the school's current NERDC scheme of work and state/school offerings before writing question sets.

JSS 1 is treated as a common foundational level rather than separate science, arts, and commercial streams. Subjects appear once, with strand tags to make the three requested areas easy to distinguish without duplicating questions.

## Proposed subject coverage

| Subject section | Requested area tag(s) | Notes for question writing |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Science / Core | Foundational number, measurement, geometry, algebraic thinking, and data handling at JSS 1 level. |
| Basic Science and Technology | Science | Keep science and technology strands identifiable in the section; confirm the school's subject naming and current scheme. |
| Digital Technologies / ICT | Science / Commercial | Digital literacy, safe computer use, and introductory productivity concepts; confirm the school's current title. |
| Agricultural Science / Agricultural Technology | Science / Commercial | Include only if part of the school's JSS 1 scheme; use age-appropriate local agriculture examples. |
| English Studies | Arts / Humanities / Core | Reading, grammar, vocabulary, and writing conventions; use Nigerian English contexts without testing dialect as error. |
| Yoruba | Arts / Humanities | User-selected Nigerian language; use standard Yoruba spelling and tones where relevant. |
| Social and Citizenship Studies | Arts / Humanities | Confirm the current subject title and scheme; focus on community, citizenship, and social responsibilities. |
| Nigerian History | Arts / Humanities | Include according to the current school/state scheme and use historically accurate, level-appropriate material. |
| Cultural and Creative Arts | Arts / Humanities | Art, music, drama, dance, and Nigerian cultural heritage; avoid treating any one tradition as universal. |
| Physical and Health Education | Science / Humanities | Health, safety, fitness, and sports knowledge; distinguish factual assessment from practical skills. |
| Business Studies | Commercial | Introductory trade, office, enterprise, and bookkeeping concepts appropriate to JSS 1. |
| Christian Religious Studies (CRS) | Arts / Humanities | A separate optional subject bank; do not combine its questions with Islamic Studies. |
| Islamic Studies | Arts / Humanities | A separate optional subject bank; do not combine its questions with CRS. |

## Coverage rules

- Draft a distinct set of at least 100 objective questions for each subject actually offered at the target school/level. The two religious-studies banks remain separate alternatives.
- Attach each question to one subject and one topic; balance recall, understanding, and simple application.
- Every question has four options, exactly one defensible answer, and a concise explanation in the answer guide.
- Do not silently include optional subjects in a school's edition. The final document should state which optional language, religion, and practical/vocational subjects it covers.
- Validate exact course titles and term/topic coverage with current school materials before claiming strict scheme-of-work alignment.
